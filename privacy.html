SideOut — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

SideOut ("the app") is a recreational volleyball team generator, scoreboard and session organiser published by Flux Developments ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we handle and why.

Accounts

You can use SideOut as a guest without signing in. If you choose to save your profile, you can create an account using your email address or Sign in with Apple. We store your account identifier and, if provided, your email address so you can sign back in on another device. Authentication and account storage are handled by our backend provider, Supabase.

Data we store

When you use online features (creating or joining a session, or saving your profile to an account) we store the following on our backend so it can sync between devices and to other players in your session:

  • Your display name and chosen playing positions
  • Your avatar colour and, if you add one, your profile photo
  • Your optional Instagram handle, motto and shirt number
  • Your SideOut level and the results that moved it
  • Session information: the join code and the list of players who joined
  • Match results you were part of, so they reach everyone who played
  • Clubs and teams you belong to, your role in them, and your replies to their training and fixtures
  • Comments you post on a club session
  • Coaching notes written about you by your team's staff, and notes you write about others if you coach
  • A notification token for each device you use, so we can send you a push about a new session or a note
  • Your email address, if you create an email account

Players who join a session — including via the web join page, without the app — can see the names, positions and photos of others in that same session. Profile photos are stored in a publicly accessible bucket, meaning the image can be viewed by anyone who has its link.

Who can see what

Players in a session — including anyone who joined from the web page without the app — can see the names, positions, shirt numbers and photos of others in that same session. People in your club can see your name and your replies to its sessions.

Coaching notes are private to your team's coaching staff unless the person who wrote one chooses to share it with the player it is about. A player can always read the notes that have been shared with them, and never sees the ones that haven't.

Data stored only on your device

Tournaments you run, any match in progress, and your badges are kept on your device and are not sent to us. Using "Reset profile & data" in the Profile screen removes local data; deleting the app removes it too.

Deleting your account or your data

You can delete your account and everything associated with it at any time from the Account screen in the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. You can also remove individual things — your photo, your match history, a comment, a club membership — without deleting your account.

If you would rather we did it, email sideout@fluxdevelopments.co.uk from the address on your account. We confirm and delete within 30 days, usually much sooner. Routine encrypted backups are overwritten on a rolling 30-day cycle, after which no copy remains.

Camera and photos

Camera access is used only to scan SideOut QR codes for joining sessions. Photo-library access is used only if you choose to set a profile picture. Camera frames are processed on-device and are not recorded.

Third parties

We use Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) as our backend processor. We use Apple for Sign in with Apple. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell your data. If you tap an Instagram handle you are taken to Instagram, whose own policy then applies.

Children

SideOut is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: sideout@fluxdevelopments.co.uk
